## Brief

Create simple 2D-game, where your goal is to deliver a ball to the exit zone. The ball is affected by gravity and moving through the labyrinth with walls and traps. The player controls by rotating THE LEVEL (i.e. he IS NOT controlling the ball directly). Rotation should be smooth, not just +/- 90 degrees.

## Tech-stack

- HTML5, TypeScript, CSS
- SVG for graphics
- Local Storage
- NPM
- Vite
- JSON for levels and configuration

## Content

- 10 levels
- Ball
- Common walls
- Goal zone
- Sticky walls
- Bouncy walls
- Accelerating zones
- Braking zones
- Collectable coins
- Collectable notes revealing mystery story
- Spike traps
- Moving traps
- Level editor
- Highscores
